# Reflection

The most difficult concept in this course was **scaling systems under heavy load**. At first, I struggled to understand how caches, replicas, and queues fit together without breaking consistency. I overcame this by carefully studying examples, drawing diagrams, and practicing with smaller projects like QuickNotes. Seeing how each component solved a specific bottleneck helped me connect the theory to practice.

In my capstone, the part I would improve is the **data model for tags and note relationships**. Feedback showed that while my schema worked, it could be optimized with better indexing and constraints. I would revisit the design to ensure queries remain efficient as the dataset grows.

Next, I want to learn more about **cloud-native deployments**: containerization with Docker, orchestration with Kubernetes, and monitoring tools. These skills will help me take the architectural designs I’ve learned and actually run them at scale in production environments. By combining strong design principles with practical DevOps knowledge, I’ll be able to build systems that are not only well-designed but also resilient and maintainable.
